## 11. Revisions

This project includes {{REVISION_ROUNDS}} rounds of revision per Deliverable.

A **revision** is a change that brings a Deliverable closer to what was already agreed: correcting an error, adjusting spacing or colour within the agreed design, rewording supplied content, or fixing behaviour that does not match clause 4, 5 or 6.

**New scope** is anything else: a new feature, a new page, a new integration, a change of direction on an approved design, or a requirement that appears for the first time after signature. New scope is not a revision, however small it seems, and is handled under clause 12.

Revision rounds that are not used are not refundable and cannot be exchanged for other work.

Where the Client's feedback within an included round mixes revisions and new scope, Complex AI will do the revisions and raise a Change Request for the rest, rather than silently absorbing it or silently billing for it.

## 12. Change Requests

Any change to the scope, the deliverables, the timeline or the fees in this Statement of Work is recorded in a Change Request.

A Change Request describes the change, the reason for it, the additional cost, the effect on the timeline, and the effect on existing features. It is issued in the form of the Complex AI Change Request document and refers to this Statement of Work by its project reference.

**Complex AI will not begin work covered by a Change Request until the Client has approved it, and the Client is not liable for the cost of that work until it has approved it.** A signed Change Request amends this Statement of Work.

## 13. Testing and Acceptance

When Complex AI considers a Deliverable complete, it will notify the Client and make it available for testing.

The Client has {{ACCEPTANCE_PERIOD}} from that notice to test the Deliverable against clauses 4, 5 and 6, and to either accept it in writing or give Complex AI a single written list of defects.

A **defect** is a failure of the Deliverable to work as described in clauses 4, 5 and 6. A request for something not described there is new scope and clause 12 applies.

Complex AI will correct reported defects at no charge and resubmit the Deliverable. The acceptance period then runs again for the corrected items only.

A Deliverable is treated as accepted if the Client accepts it in writing, if the Client does not respond within the acceptance period, or if the Client puts the Deliverable into live commercial use.

Acceptance of a Deliverable does not waive Complex AI's obligations under clause 16 of the Master Client Services Agreement.
