## 8. Intellectual Property

This clause determines who owns what. It is the clause most likely to be misunderstood, so it is set out in full.

### 8.1 Client materials

The Client owns, and continues to own, everything it supplies to Complex AI. This includes its logo, its trade marks, its photographs, text it has written, its business data, its customer data, and any other material it provides. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any of that to Complex AI.

The Client grants Complex AI a licence to use that material only for the purpose of performing the Services. That licence ends when this Agreement ends, except to the extent Complex AI must retain material to comply with the law.

### 8.2 Complex AI intellectual property

Complex AI owns, and continues to own, the technology it uses to build and operate its services. This includes:

- Its source code, including code written before or during a Project that is general in application
- Its frameworks, libraries and reusable components
- Its templates, boilerplate and starter projects
- Its website engines and content management tooling
- Its SaaS platforms and the software that runs them
- Its application programming interfaces
- Its internal tools, scripts and infrastructure
- Its development methodologies, know-how and technical documentation
- Any general-purpose software, and any intellectual property that existed before the relevant Project began

**Paying for a service does not transfer ownership of this technology.** When the Client buys a website, a subscription or a custom build, it is buying the delivered result and the right to use it. It is not buying the underlying platform, the reusable components, or the general-purpose code that Complex AI uses across its clients. Complex AI remains free to use that technology for other clients and for its own purposes.

### 8.3 Licence to the Client

On payment of all amounts due for the relevant Deliverable, Complex AI grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use that Deliverable for its own business purposes, including the right to have it hosted, maintained and modified on its behalf.

Where the Deliverable is a SaaS subscription, the licence is instead a right to access and use the software for the duration of the subscription, and it ends when the subscription ends.

### 8.4 Transfer of ownership

If the Client requires ownership of custom-developed work rather than a licence, that must be stated expressly in the applicable Statement of Work, together with any additional fee. Any such transfer:

- Applies only to the specific custom work identified in the Statement of Work
- Takes effect only once all amounts owing under that Statement of Work have been paid in full
- Does not extend to the pre-existing, reusable or general-purpose technology described in clause 8.2, which Complex AI licenses to the Client to the extent needed to use the transferred work

### 8.5 Portfolio use

Complex AI may identify the Client as a client and show the visible result of the work in its portfolio and marketing, unless the Client objects in writing. This does not permit Complex AI to disclose Confidential Information.
