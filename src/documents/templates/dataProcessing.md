- Access to production systems is limited to people who need it for their work
- Access is authenticated, and administrative access is separately controlled

- Data in transit between the Client, Complex AI and its infrastructure providers is encrypted
- Infrastructure and application dependencies are kept reasonably up to date with security patches
- Access to systems is logged
- Security incidents are handled in accordance with clause 10

## 10. Security Compromises

Where Complex AI becomes aware of a security compromise affecting Personal Information it processes for the Client, it will:

- Notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of it
- Provide the information it has about what happened, what Personal Information is affected, and which data subjects are likely to be affected
- Take reasonable steps to contain the compromise and limit the harm
- Keep the Client informed as it learns more
- Cooperate reasonably with the Client's own response, including any notification the Client must make

Notifications required by law, including any notification to the Information Regulator or to affected data subjects, are made in accordance with the requirements of the Protection of Personal Information Act and within the timeframes it prescribes. As responsible party, the Client is ordinarily the party that makes those notifications, and Complex AI will give it the information and assistance it reasonably needs to do so.

Complex AI will not notify a regulator or a data subject about a compromise affecting the Client's Personal Information without first consulting the Client, unless the law requires it to.

> ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED: the notification obligations in this clause, and the division of responsibility for notifying the Information Regulator and affected data subjects. Confirm the statutory requirements and whether any specific timeframe should be written into this clause.

## 16. Audit and Cooperation

Complex AI will make available to the Client, on reasonable written request, the information the Client reasonably needs to satisfy itself that Complex AI is meeting its obligations under this agreement.

Where that information is not sufficient, the Client may request an audit. An audit:

- May be requested no more than once in any twelve-month period, unless a security compromise or a regulator requires otherwise
- Requires at least 20 Business Days' written notice
- Takes place during business hours and in a way that does not unreasonably disrupt Complex AI's operations or affect other clients
- Is subject to the confidentiality obligations in clause 10 of the Master Client Services Agreement
- Does not extend to information about other clients, or to Complex AI's own commercially sensitive technical information beyond what is needed to verify compliance
- Is at the Client's cost, unless it reveals a material failure by Complex AI to meet its obligations

## 17. Client Responsibilities

The Client is responsible for its own compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act as responsible party. In particular the Client will:

- Collect Personal Information lawfully, and have a lawful basis for the processing it instructs Complex AI to carry out
- Give data subjects the notices required of it
- Obtain consent where consent is the basis it relies on, and manage the withdrawal of consent
- Give Complex AI accurate and lawful instructions
- Ensure that Personal Information it provides to Complex AI is accurate and no more than is needed for the purpose
- Keep its own accounts, credentials and access secure, and manage who within its organisation can see Personal Information
- Deal with data subject requests, with Complex AI's assistance under clause 12
- Tell Complex AI in advance if it intends to have special personal information or children's information processed

Complex AI is not responsible for the Client's compliance with the law, and this agreement does not state the full extent of the Client's obligations under the Protection of Personal Information Act. The Client should take its own advice on those.
