## 11. Approval

**Complex AI will not begin any work described in this Change Request until the Client has approved it in writing. The Client is not liable for the cost of that work until it has approved it.**

Work already covered by the Statement of Work continues in the meantime, unless this change makes that impractical, in which case Complex AI will say so before pausing anything.

This Change Request lapses if it is not approved within 30 days of its date, after which the cost and the timeline may need to be re-estimated.

Approval may be given by signing below, or by written confirmation from the Client's representative by email, which the Parties agree has the same effect. Clause 24 of the Master Client Services Agreement governs electronic signature and electronic communication.

> ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED: this clause allows a signed agreement to be amended by email. Clause 24 of the Master Client Services Agreement requires amendments to be in writing and signed, and also permits electronic signature. Confirm that these two provisions work together, and that an email approval is enough to vary a signed Statement of Work.
